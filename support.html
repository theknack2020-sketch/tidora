Tidora: Swipe Photo Cleaner

How the App Works

How does swiping work?

Tidora shows your photos one at a time as cards. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, or swipe up to favorite (adds to your iOS Favorites album — a power gesture you can enable in Settings).

Your delete decisions are held in a basket until the end of the round. Nothing is deleted until you review the basket and tap Confirm Delete. You stay in control at every step.

Can I undo a swipe?

Yes. Tap the Undo button (↩) during a round to return the last card to the queue. Free users can undo within the current session. Pro users get unlimited undo history — you can step back through an entire round.

At the end of every round you also see a full review grid of all items in the delete basket, so you can remove anything before confirming.

How does batch delete work?

As you swipe left, deleted items are collected silently into a basket — there's no interruption mid-round. When the round ends, Tidora shows you a summary screen with a grid of everything in the basket. You can:

  • Tap any photo in the grid to remove it from the basket (put it back to "keep")
  • Tap Confirm Delete to move the whole basket to iOS Recently Deleted
  • Tap Cancel to keep everything and start fresh

The actual deletion happens only when you tap Confirm Delete.

I deleted photos but my storage didn't free up — why?

When Tidora deletes photos, iOS moves them to the Recently Deleted album inside the Photos app. They stay there for 30 days before iOS permanently removes them and reclaims the storage.

To free storage right away:

  • Open the Photos app on your iPhone
  • Go to Albums → Recently Deleted
  • Tap Delete All (top-left) to permanently remove everything now
Once you empty Recently Deleted, those photos are gone permanently and cannot be recovered. Tidora shows a reminder about this after every round.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free tier: 50 swipes per day (resets at midnight), month-by-month rounds, On This Day rounds, Screenshots sweep, Large Files sweep, undo within a session.

Pro tier: Unlimited swipes, full Smart Sweeps (duplicates + keep-the-best), unlimited undo history, advanced stats and decision speed/ratio personality, win-back offer eligibility.

No ads in either tier — ever.

What is the daily swipe limit?

Free users can swipe 50 photos per day. The counter resets at midnight local time. Kept photos and undone swipes both count toward the limit. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited swipes.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Tidora subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple. To cancel:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions
  • Find Tidora Pro and tap Cancel Subscription

You retain Pro access until the end of the current billing period. TheKnack cannot cancel subscriptions on your behalf.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open Tidora → go to Settings (Me tab) → tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID you used to purchase. If restore fails, check your internet connection and try again.

How does the duplicates / keep-the-best feature work?

Tidora's Smart Sweeps (Pro) scans for visually similar photos using on-device Vision analysis — no photos leave your device. Similar photos are grouped into a single card. Tidora scores each photo on sharpness, exposure, and whether eyes are open, then suggests the best one to keep. You can override the suggestion before sending the rest to the basket.

The analysis runs progressively in the background while the app is open; the Smart Sweeps tab shows a progress indicator as it fills.

What about iCloud Photos "Optimize Storage"?

If you use iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage enabled, your device may hold low-resolution thumbnails while originals live in iCloud. When Tidora shows you a photo to review, it requests the full-resolution version from iCloud if needed (requires Wi-Fi or cellular).

When you delete a photo through Tidora, it is removed from your iCloud Photos library — which means it disappears from all devices sharing that library (iPhone, iPad, Mac). The deletion does not just free local device storage; it removes the item from the cloud library entirely.

Tidora labels the space reclaimed as "library size" and does not make promises about immediate local device-storage recovery, since iCloud Optimize manages local storage automatically.

Is my data private? Does Tidora upload my photos?

No — your photos never leave your device. All processing (grouping, scoring, Vision analysis) runs entirely on-device. Tidora never uploads photos, thumbnails, filenames, or any photo-derived data to any server.

The only data that leaves your device is anonymous, non-linked crash and usage analytics via TelemetryDeck (no IDFA, no personal data). You can turn this off in Settings → Data & Privacy → Analytics.

Does Tidora work on iPad?

Yes. Tidora is a Universal app and supports iPhone and iPad. On iPad, Tidora uses a split-layout view with a sidebar for navigation and the swipe deck in the main area. Keyboard shortcuts are also supported: ← arrow key to delete, → arrow key to keep.

Troubleshooting

Photo permission denied — app shows no photos

Tidora needs access to your photo library to work. If you denied permission at first launch:

  • Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos
  • Find Tidora and select All Photos
  • Return to Tidora — it will load your library automatically

Limited Photos access (selected photos only) reduces what Tidora can show you. Full access is recommended for the best experience.

App feels slow or stuttery on a very large library

Tidora is designed for libraries up to 100K+ assets, but initial indexing can take a moment on first launch. The month map and first round should appear within seconds. Smart Sweeps analysis runs in the background and fills progressively — a "Preparing…" badge shows progress. If the app feels sluggish:

  • Force-quit and relaunch Tidora (double-tap Home or swipe up → swipe the app away)
  • Make sure your device has at least a few hundred MB of free storage for temporary processing
  • On older devices, Smart Sweeps analysis may take longer — this is normal

Video won't preview in the card

Tidora auto-plays a silent 3-second loop of videos in the card view. If a video shows a static thumbnail instead:

  • Check that the video is fully downloaded from iCloud (look for a cloud icon — tap it to download)
  • Ensure you have a network connection if iCloud Optimize Storage is on
  • If the video is in a format not supported by iOS (rare), the static thumbnail is a fallback

A photo looks different in Tidora than in the Photos app

Tidora displays the original captured image. If you applied edits in the Photos app (crop, filters, adjustments), Tidora shows the original — not the edited version — for an accurate quality assessment. Deletions always act on the original + edits together (iOS deletes the full asset).

Daily reminder notifications aren't firing

Tidora uses local notifications for daily sweep reminders. If you're not receiving them:

  • Open Settings → Notifications → Tidora and confirm notifications are allowed
  • Check that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not blocking the notification
  • Inside Tidora, go to Settings → Reminder and confirm the time is set correctly
  • Toggle the reminder off and back on to re-schedule it
