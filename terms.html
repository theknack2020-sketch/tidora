6. Photo Deletions — Your Responsibility

Important — please read carefully: When you confirm a deletion in Tidora, photos and videos are moved to the iOS Recently Deleted album. They remain there for 30 days, after which iOS permanently and irrecoverably deletes them. Tidora is not liable for photos permanently deleted after the 30-day Recently Deleted window has expired.

How deletion works

  • Tidora collects your swipe decisions into a basket. You review the basket before any deletion is confirmed.
  • You tap "Confirm Delete" to proceed. This triggers a standard iOS system call that moves items to Recently Deleted.
  • You have 30 days to recover items from the iOS Photos app (Albums → Recently Deleted) or by tapping "Empty Trash" inside Photos to free storage sooner.
  • After 30 days, iOS permanently removes the items. This is an iOS system behavior outside Tidora's control.

iCloud Photos — "Optimize Storage"

If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage" enabled, deleting a photo in Tidora removes it from your iCloud library, not merely from local device storage. The deletion propagates to all devices sharing the same iCloud Photos library. Please ensure you understand your iCloud Photos configuration before confirming deletions.

User responsibility

You are solely responsible for reviewing items in the basket before confirming deletion. Tidora provides an undo button during a round and a full review grid before the final confirmation step. Use these safeguards. TheKnack is not responsible for any loss of photos or videos resulting from your use of the deletion features.
