Tidora: Clean Photos by Month

Tidora is built on a simple promise: your photos never leave your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photo library on any server. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad, under your control.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tidora ("the app", "we", "us") accesses on your device, why none of it is collected or transmitted, and what we do not do. Tidora is developed and operated by TheKnack.

By using Tidora, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it.

2. What We Access — Your Photo Library

Tidora requests access to your device photo library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription) to show you photos and videos for review. Specifically:

  • We read photo metadata (date, file size, media type) to group and present photos by month or category.
  • We display photo thumbnails and full images on-screen so you can decide to keep or delete them.
  • When you confirm a deletion, Tidora asks iOS to move those items to your device's Recently Deleted album (a standard iOS system call — PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets).
  • On-device Vision analysis (sharpness, exposure scoring for duplicate groups) runs entirely on your device's Neural Engine.
Critical: No photo content, thumbnail, filename, GPS metadata, or any derivative of your photos is ever uploaded, transmitted, or shared. The photo library access stays 100% on-device.

3. What We Collect — Nothing

Tidora collects no data at all. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no account system and no server: the app has no way to send us anything. The App Store privacy label for Tidora reads Data Not Collected, and the app’s bundled privacy manifest declares an empty collection list to match.

Earlier versions of Tidora shipped an anonymous analytics SDK (TelemetryDeck). It was removed entirely in version 2.0, along with the in-app analytics toggle that used to control it — there is nothing left to switch off.

Specifically, Tidora does not collect

  • No photos, filenames, or any photo content
  • No usage or product-interaction events
  • No crash or diagnostic reports
  • No name, email address, or any contact information
  • No device identifier (no IDFA)
  • No location data
  • No cross-app or cross-website tracking

Everything Tidora knows — your months, your progress, your milestones — is stored on your device and never leaves it.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising — Tidora contains zero ads. Ever.
  • No ad SDKs — no AdMob, no AppLovin, no any advertising framework
  • No user accounts — no sign-up, no email, no password, no profile
  • No data sale — we do not sell, rent, or trade any data
  • No third-party tracking — no Facebook SDK, no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel
  • No cross-app tracking — Tidora does not track you across other apps or websites
  • No server-side processing of your photos — zero uploads, zero inference in the cloud
  • No hidden album access — Tidora never reads your Hidden album

5. Data Storage & Retention

All app data created by Tidora — your swipe history, session progress, statistics, milestones, streak, and settings — is stored on your device only using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults.

  • Data stays on your device; it is not synced to iCloud in version 1.0.
  • Data is never transmitted to any server.
  • Deleting Tidora from your device permanently removes all Tidora-generated data.

PHAsset identifiers (references to photos) are stored locally only as index references. Photo content itself is always read directly from the iOS photo library and is never copied or cached by Tidora.

6. Photo Deletions & Recently Deleted

When you confirm a deletion in Tidora, iOS moves those items to the system Recently Deleted album. Items remain there for 30 days and can be recovered via the Photos app before that window closes. After 30 days, iOS permanently deletes them. Tidora has no control over this window — it is an iOS system behavior. Please see our Terms of Use for the liability section on permanent deletion.

7. Children's Privacy

Tidora is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Others)

Because Tidora collects no personal data linked to you, most traditional data-subject requests (access, portability, erasure) are structurally satisfied by design — there is no personal data on our servers to access, export, or delete.

GDPR (EU / EEA Users)

Tidora performs no processing of personal data, so no lawful basis under the GDPR is engaged: there is no collection, no transmission and no third-party recipient. Should you wish to raise a complaint regardless, you may contact the data protection supervisory authority in your country.

CCPA (California Users)

Tidora does not sell personal information, and it collects none in the first place. You have the right to know what is collected — this policy is that disclosure, and the answer is nothing.

Exercising your rights

There is no analytics to opt out of. To delete all local Tidora data, uninstall the app. For any other inquiry, contact us at theknack2020@gmail.com.

9. Third-Party Services

Tidora uses the following third-party services:

Apple (StoreKit / App Store)

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. Apple handles payment information; Tidora never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy. Subscription management is handled entirely in your App Store account settings.

None

Tidora bundles no third-party SDKs. The only external party involved is Apple, which processes your purchase if you subscribe — Tidora never sees your payment details.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics providers, ad networks, or data brokers are used.

10. Security

Since Tidora does not transmit your photos or personal data to any server, the attack surface is minimal. App data stored on your device is protected by iOS's built-in data protection mechanisms (FileProtection). We follow Apple's security best practices and keep the dependency footprint small.
